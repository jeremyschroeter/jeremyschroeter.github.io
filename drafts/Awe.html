I don't think there is any more powerful feeling than awe. Sure, other things may make us freeze up, or stop, but awe is not mere surprise or shock. It's not your friends arriving unannounced, and it isn't receiving a devastating diagnosis over the phone. It is both a deep sense of smallness and an almost umbilical connection to that enoromous 'something' staring back at you.

This last Summer, my friend Jackson and I got up to some hiking back home in Washington State, before I turned to New Jersey. Though much of the West is on fire right now, my timing was perfect, and Jackson and I enjoyed the state unabated.

One trip we went on was a 3 day backpacking trip to Toleak Point. Who is noticing the experience if not thta thing having the experience? Are there two of me in me? Are tehre an infinite of me in me? Or am I simply one thing, confused by the ability or my focus to be completely preiccupied by cognitive directives setup to optimize not for my success, but for whatever its foals are.
